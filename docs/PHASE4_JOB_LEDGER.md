# Phase-4 job ledger

Every GPU job of the Phase-4 runs. One NVIDIA RTX 5000 Ada (32 GB) ran all of them; up to 8 jobs (round 1) and 9 jobs (round 2) shared the GPU, so the durations are not single-job speeds.

- Generated by `scripts/p4_ledger.py` from the queue ledgers `logs/phase4_queue/<mode>/status.tsv`. Those, and the per-queue console logs, stay local (gitignored).
- Each job's own transcript is committed as `run*.log` next to its summaries, in the directory under `results/phase4/` given in the *Output* column.
- Exit 0 means the job finished and wrote its summary.
